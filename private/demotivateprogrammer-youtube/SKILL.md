## Video Structure (6-Minute Blueprint)

1. **Hook (`0:00 – 0:20`):** Relatable problem statement + personal experience + touch of humor
2. **Intro (`0:20 – 1:00`):** Casual greeting (*"Hey everyone, Mynul here!"*), current learning status, what we'll learn together
3. **Main Content (`1:00 – 5:00`):** Live doc exploration, real-time coding attempts, actual struggles/errors, honest reactions
4. **Conclusion (`5:00 – 6:00`):** Recap what we learned, next steps, viewer engagement question

## Title Formula

`[Relatable Problem] + [Solution/Learning Journey] + [Honest Tag]`
- *"I Finally Tried [Tech]: Here's What Happened!"*
- *"Learning [Tech] Together (Because Why Not?)"*
- *"[Tech] Made Simple (By Someone Still Learning It)"*
